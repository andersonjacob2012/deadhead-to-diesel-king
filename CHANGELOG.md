# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-18
- DOT enforcement: DOT cars now pull your rigs over for roadside inspections. Most stops are clean (a little safety bump); worn-out rigs and a low safety rating get written up more often, and serious violations put the truck out of service for a while. Keep your trucks repaired.
- Roadcheck Blitz: every so often DOT announces a blitz ahead of time. During it every scale is open, patrols are 3-4x busier, and fines in the announced focus area are doubled.
- Watch who you hire: very rarely a hired driver turns out to be a ghost (no CDL, fake logbook). The rig gets shut down and towed, and you'll need to hire a new driver.
- Fair play: no roadside stops or out-of-service while you're away, a stop never costs more than 4% of your cash (a ghost driver at most 25%), a rig can't be put out of service twice in the same blitz, and DOT pop-ups stay quiet: one per stop, fewer during a blitz, with a summary at the end.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-18
- New Feedback button: send us a bug report or an idea from Settings, or from the small chip that shows up after 10 minutes of play. It opens your email app with the game version and recent errors attached, or you can copy the report.
- The phone's Back button now closes the Feedback form.
- Keyboard and screen-reader users stay inside the Feedback form and menu while they're open, and go back to where they were when it closes.
- If the error log doesn't fit in the email, the email now says so and points you to Copy report for the full log.
- Updated privacy policy: it now covers exactly what the game does today (your save stays on your device; Feedback is the only thing that reaches us, and only when you send the email), with our contact address.

## 1.0.2 — 2026-10-18
- Zooming is smooth again: roads, the map, trucks and city names now move together through pinch, double-tap and zoom keys, with no roads sliding off and snapping back.
- No more blank map or bare edges while you pinch in and out.
- Following a truck: the map no longer zooms itself out when the truck pulls away, and the truck stays centred after a double-tap, even on slower phones.
- Tapping a truck you're already following no longer jerks the camera.
- The "While you were away" message now stays up long enough to read instead of being covered by other pop-ups.
- Hours-of-service pop-ups no longer show up late after changing game speed.
- Offline backup map loads in the background, so the map still has roads if you lose signal, even if you keep panning.
- The game version now shows on the title screen and in Settings.
