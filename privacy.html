The short version

  • We don’t use any analytics service yet. We don’t sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account and all cloud data in the Game (Settings → Account → Delete account) or at our deletion page.

3. Ads, tracking and your choices

  • iOS: we ask for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before AdMob can use your IDFA. If you decline, ads are non-personalized. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • EEA/UK/Switzerland: we show Google’s consent message before personalized ads. You can change your choice from Settings → Privacy in the Game.
  • The web version of the Game currently shows no ads.

4. How long we keep data

  • Account and cloud save: until you delete your account. We also keep the last 5 versions of your save for recovery, and they are deleted along with it.
  • Guest accounts that are never linked and have been inactive for [e.g. 12 months] may be deleted automatically.
  • Fair-play flags are stored with your save and deleted with it.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple/Google and RevenueCat as needed for tax, accounting and refund obligations, even after account deletion.
  • Backups maintained by our database provider roll off within [e.g. 7 days].

5. Deleting your data

In the Game: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, email address, cloud saves, save history and fair-play flags. Without the app, use this page to email a deletion request. “Reset save” only restarts the game; it does not delete your account.
